Handloom craft, still living
03The signature

Woven light,
carried far.

Known for their fine cotton, elegant zari borders and feather-light feel, Venkatagiri sarees are one of Andhra Pradesh’s most treasured handloom traditions.

Behind every saree is a rhythm: warp, weft, patience and a maker’s eye. The craft remains part of the town’s identity and its future.
